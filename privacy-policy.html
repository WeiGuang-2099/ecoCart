EcoCart Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

1. Information We Collect

EcoCart follows the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and collects only the minimum information required to power the product:

  • Product barcodes used to match carbon-footprint data
  • No personally identifiable information such as name, email, or phone number
  • Uploaded photos are processed in-memory and discarded immediately after analysis
  • Location data is used only when you explicitly allow it for nearby eco-store recommendations

2. How We Use Data

  • Barcodes are queried against Australian sustainability databases only
  • Images are used exclusively for AI recognition and never repurposed
  • We do not sell or rent any data to third parties
  • We do not build advertising or behavioral profiles

3. Data Security

  • All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS
  • Image data stays in volatile memory - no persistent storage
  • We follow Australian cyber-security best practices
  • Security reviews are performed on a regular cadence

4. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act you may:

  • Request access to the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Lodge a complaint regarding our handling of personal data

5. International Data Transfers

EcoCart operates primarily within Australia. Some AI recognition services may rely on infrastructure outside Australia, and we ensure any transfer meets the Privacy Act requirements.
